What we do

Three ways we support a child's education.

1

Scholarships

From school awards to undergraduate support, we help meritorious students stay in education — and ask them to give back to society in turn.

2

Stationery Kits

We identify what each child needs and provide adequate stationery materials across classes — over 2,000 kits distributed to date.

3

Computer Literacy

We collect, service, and install donated computers in government schools, and train teachers to carry computer education forward.
