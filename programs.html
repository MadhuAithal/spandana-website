Programs

Designed around a child's needs.

Our programs are carefully designed for different age groups — from foundational support to undergraduate scholarships.

Merit Scholarships

Scholarships support meritorious students throughout their undergraduate education, helping children from government schools realise their educational dreams.

Academic Awards

Every year we recognise students who excel academically — class toppers and subject toppers after their 7th and 10th standard exams. Awards include a certificate of excellence, a cash award, a stationery kit, and a motivational book.

Ekalavya Scholarship

Physical health and well-being matter too. Through the Ekalavya scholarship we encourage students — boys and girls nominated by their school — to excel in sports and physical activities.

Kala Samrat Scholarship

We recognise talent in the arts as well, awarding the Kala Samrat scholarship to students who excel in creative and cultural fields.

Stationery Distribution

We identify the needs of children and provide adequate stationery materials across classes. Over 2,000 stationery kits have been distributed to date.

Computer Literacy

We collect used computers from institutions and individuals, service them, and install them in government schools — and train teachers in basic and advanced computing so they can teach the children.
